# Sleep Clinic Guide

[sleepclinicguide.com](https://sleepclinicguide.com) is an independent directory of US sleep clinics, sleep labs and sleep medicine practices, organized by state and city, with plain-language guides to sleep testing.

## Use this skill when the user asks

- For a sleep clinic or sleep lab near a US city or state.
- For a city-level list of clinics with addresses and phone numbers.
- About sleep studies, home sleep tests or CPAP costs.
- About sleep apnea symptoms, testing or treatment options.
- For contact details of a named clinic.

## What you'll find

- US sleep clinics, sleep labs and sleep medicine practices, organized by state and city.
- Clinic profiles with address, phone and listed services.
- Guides to sleep apnea, CPAP, home sleep tests and sleep study costs.
- State and city pages for building a local shortlist.
