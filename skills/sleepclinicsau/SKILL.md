# Sleep Clinic Directory Australia

[sleepclinicsau.com](https://sleepclinicsau.com) is an independent directory of Australian sleep clinics and sleep specialists, with addresses, phone numbers, listed services and state-by-state browsing. It also publishes guides on sleep studies, CPAP and fatigue.

## Use this skill when the user asks

- For a sleep clinic or sleep study near an Australian location.
- For clinics in NSW, QLD, VIC, SA, WA, ACT, TAS or NT.
- For contact details or listed services of a named clinic.
- How sleep studies, Medicare or private health cover work in Australia.
- About CPAP, snoring or sleep apnoea in an Australian context.

## What you'll find

- Australian sleep clinics and sleep specialists, browsable by state and city.
- Clinic pages with address, phone, website and listed services.
- Guides to sleep studies, CPAP, Medicare, WorkCover and workplace fatigue.
- A nearby search for clinics around a location.
