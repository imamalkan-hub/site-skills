# Superyacht Charter Guide

[superyachtcharterguide.com](https://superyachtcharterguide.com) publishes independent, numbers-first guides to chartering a superyacht or catamaran: weekly base rates, APA, VAT, tipping, insurance, contracts and destination budgets.

## Use this skill when the user asks

- What it costs to charter a yacht for a week.
- What APA, VAT, delivery fees or crew tips add to the base rate.
- Bareboat vs crewed, catamaran vs monohull, or charter vs ownership.
- About contracts, deposits, damage waivers or cancellation cover.
- About a specific named yacht's published rate band and specs.
- Which destinations fit a budget or season.

## What you'll find

- Weekly base rates plus APA, VAT, crew tips and delivery fees, line by line.
- Bareboat vs crewed, catamaran vs monohull, and charter vs ownership.
- Contracts, deposits, damage waivers, cancellation cover and insurance.
- Destination budgets, a named yacht fleet and an all-in cost calculator.
