# Find Sleep Doctors

[findsleepdoctors.com](https://findsleepdoctors.com) is an independent directory of sleep medicine specialists and sleep clinics across six English-speaking countries. You can search by location or browse by country, region and city, then open a profile with the doctor's address, phone, website and specialties.

## Use this skill when the user asks

- For a sleep doctor or sleep specialist near a place.
- For a sleep clinic or sleep lab in a city or state.
- For a sleep medicine specialist who offers telehealth.
- To compare sleep specialists in two cities or regions.
- For contact details of a named sleep practice.
